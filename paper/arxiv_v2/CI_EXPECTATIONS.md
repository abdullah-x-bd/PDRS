# CI acceptance expectations

The revision is acceptable only when:

1. semantic unit tests pass;
2. the deterministic experiment generates 3,600 defect-study rows;
3. every coordinated allocation has zero duplicate ranks;
4. all malformed schemas are rejected;
5. the paper compiles with no overfull boxes;
6. the extended QuantLib, SimpleFIX, and ISO 20022 jobs complete;
7. generated artifacts are retained for inspection.
