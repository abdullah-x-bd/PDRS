## Research objective

Implement the full Phase 1–9 revision programme for the PDRS paper and artifact.

## Changes

- freezes exact PDRS and FinSpace source identities
- adds SciFe, Feat, knowledge compilation, constrained generation, and ACTS positioning
- formalizes typed values, well-formedness, injective lowering, bit complexity, Floyd sampling, and replay levels
- adds an independent semantics oracle and reduced-MDD baseline
- redesigns defect discovery across eight distributions and five policies
- compares five coordinated distributed allocations with heterogeneous costs
- replaces the Pareto and win/loss narrative with paired campaign-level bootstrap intervals
- extends QuantLib, SimpleFIX, and ISO 20022 boundary and mutation testing
- adds a pinned analysis environment, Dockerfile, one-command reproduction, checksums, manuscript, and CI artifacts

## Claim changes

The revision positions PDRS as a systems realization of established indexed dependent enumeration. It removes universal bug-finding and Pareto-frontier claims and preserves direct limitations.
