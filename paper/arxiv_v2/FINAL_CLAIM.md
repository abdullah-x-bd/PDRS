# Final defensible claim

PDRS provides a language-neutral systems realization of indexed finite-domain enumeration. It combines an external declarative schema, canonical intermediate representation, independent Python, C, and Rust runtimes, exact schema-relative object identities, and reproducible campaign primitives. Its strengths lie in exact indexing, duplicate-free assignment, and operational reproducibility. Targeted, adaptive, interaction-covering, and stochastic methods remain preferable when their corresponding objectives govern the workflow.
