# Note to reviewers

This revision deliberately narrows the contribution. The paper treats dependent indexed enumeration as established prior work and evaluates PDRS as a language-neutral systems realization with an external schema, canonical IR, independent runtimes, campaign primitives, and a finance-oriented deployment layer.

The revision retains negative findings. Targeted generators outperform object-uniform no-replacement sampling under several defect distributions. Generic coordinated allocation policies also eliminate overlap, and contiguous rank intervals can produce poor execution balance. These results define the conditions under which PDRS is useful instead of asserting universal superiority.
