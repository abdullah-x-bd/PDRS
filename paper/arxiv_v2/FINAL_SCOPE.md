# Final scope

The arXiv v2 paper advances one central systems claim:

> PDRS is a language-neutral realization of indexed finite-domain enumeration that combines an external schema, canonical intermediate representation, independent runtimes, exact schema-relative object identities, and reproducible campaign primitives.

The paper does not claim new underlying ranking mathematics, universal bug-finding superiority, unique ability to coordinate workers, production cryptography, rank stability under arbitrary edits, or a newly confirmed finance-library defect.
